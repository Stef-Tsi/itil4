# ITIL 4 Foundation question review

Reviewed on 5 October 2026. Active revision: `strict-v4-20261005`.

All 144 questions in revision 3 were read for correctness, ambiguity, plausible distractors, English feedback, syllabus coverage and question style. Content or explanations changed in 86 of those items. Another 24 original questions fill gaps, giving 168 active items.

This is an editorial assessment against published Foundation material. The questions have not been calibrated against candidate performance or the confidential live exam bank. Matching a blueprint does not establish equal difficulty or predict a first-attempt pass.

## Findings and changes

- Many distractors were unrelated to the stem or plainly unreasonable. Revised options use closer confusions: incident restoration versus cause investigation; problem control versus error control; request approval versus change authorization; provider outputs versus consumer outcomes; and component metrics versus end-to-end quality.
- Correct options were often noticeably longer. Wording was reviewed for comparable structure and detail. Among non-list items, mean option lengths now range from 52.5 to 54.8 characters across the four authoring positions. Length balance is a quality check, not evidence of real-exam difficulty. Answers are shuffled for each run.
- Coverage lacked a question on the Deliver and support activity and on the organization concept. New questions cover these, governance, SVS inputs, activity transformations, problem control, supplier information and improvement participation, standard-change procedure revisions, incident-tool support and balanced service measures.
- Item 109's explanation of the incorrect pair 2 and 4 was inaccurate: statement 4 concerns missing incident records, not dependence on cause investigation. The explanation now identifies each error separately.
- Goods versus resource access now tests the ownership distinction, rather than using unrelated organization and role labels as distractors.
- Repeated practice-purpose feedback was cleaned up. Every option still has its own English rationale, retained with the shuffled option in session and result snapshots.
- Remaining chapter names and descriptive metadata were made English.

The bank includes 147 standard, 16 list, 3 missing-word and 2 exceptional negative items. These are bank counts, not a claimed official distribution of question styles. Each list question presents four statements and four paired options with one correct pair.

Timed exams use 60 minutes, one mark per question, a pass mark of 26/40 and no negative marking. The 75-minute option is labelled for candidates granted extra time. Feedback is withheld until submission. Earlier scores and unfinished sessions are not treated as evidence for this revised bank; progress and archived records are retained.

## Reference comparison

The community quiz supplied by the user contains 174 items and feedback for each chosen option. It usefully emphasizes nearby terminology and concise stems. Its code selects 40 questions randomly, reveals the topic and provides immediate feedback; this is useful study behaviour, but does not enforce this app's timed-exam criterion allocation. Its data also contains the older name "change control". The active bank uses "change enablement", as in the checked syllabus.

No reference questions or explanations were imported. No normalized question stems match the supplied community bank exactly. This check does not establish originality by itself; the additions and revisions were independently written.
